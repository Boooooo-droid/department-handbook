A applying

form hb-1 · how applying works

how applying works

the whole thing happens on fomies.family. no wallet connection, no signing, no fee. you walk in, talk to the clerk and leave a folder. here's the route.

  1. the hallway

    you start in a basement hallway. there's a vent, a taped-off elevator, a bathroom and a door. the door is the one that matters.

  2. the door

    if the office is open, go in. if it's closed, it's closed. applications were paused once already and came back the next day.

  3. the office

    the clerk is at his desk. officially he's the intake officer. he has been watching you since you walked in.

  4. sign in with x

    this is how the department knows who you are. it's the normal x login on x.com. check the address bar before you type a password. it never asks for a wallet.

  5. pick an alias

    the name the clerk calls you. up to 20 characters. your x handle is the default if you don't object.

  6. the interview

    three multiple-choice questions, then one short written answer, 240 characters max. you get one of these prompts:

    • “why should we let you in? wrong answers encouraged.”
    • “what would make this a community you'd actually stick around for?”
    • “describe your online personality in one sentence.”

    there's no published answer key, and this page isn't going to guess one. be yourself. it's shorter.

  7. your classification

    your answers put you in one of six classifications, listed in the glossary. it's a personality label. nothing official says it changes your result.

  8. two addresses

    one solana address and one evm address (the 0x kind). you type or paste them. that's all. no connecting, no signing. the form is strict about format, so run them through the checker first.

  9. file it

    review, then file. you get a receipt, a case number and an id card in your folder. one active file per account.

  10. wait

    your status lives in your folder. approvals arrive in batches. in the clerk's words: “nothing will happen fast.”

the four statuses

  • pending

    filed and with the committee. this is where most people are.

  • approved

    the committee said yes. your folder hears it first.

  • waitlisted

    not yes, not no. as the department puts it, “the list is in a file.”

  • not selected

    the committee has no comment. that's their phrasing, not mine.

ringing the bell does not change your status. the bell has said so itself.

B address check

form ac-1 · address checker

address checker

the form refuses addresses that are even slightly off, and it doesn't always say why. paste yours here first. this uses the same rules as the form and tells you exactly what's wrong.

this runs on your device, not on a server. nothing you paste is sent, saved or logged. this page has no backend and no analytics, and its security policy blocks it from connecting to anything. turn on airplane mode and it still works.

try a broken example (uses burn and system addresses, not anyone's wallet. don't file them.)

a pass here means the format is right. it can't tell whose wallet it is, whether you control it, or whether you'll be approved. use an address you own and plan to keep.

the exact rules, for the curious
  • solana: only base58 characters (so no 0, O, I or l), 32 to 44 characters long, and it must decode to exactly 32 bytes.
  • evm: 0x with a small x, then exactly 40 characters from 0 to 9 and a to f. all lowercase or all uppercase is fine. mixed case must match the eip-55 checksum, which is a built-in typo check.
  • both: plain keyboard characters only. no invisible characters, no non-breaking spaces, no letters from other alphabets, and nothing over 128 characters. normal spaces, tabs and line breaks at the very start or end are trimmed. a gap in the middle is not.
  • the form saves evm addresses in checksummed form, so it may show yours back with different capitals. same address.

the rules were read from the form's own code and checked against it on more than 40,000 generated test inputs. the checksum uses a keccak-256 written into this page, no outside libraries. source and tests on github.

C points

form pt-1 · points, tasks and x activity

fomies points and activity

your folder has a page called the fomies activity record. it shows fomies points, tasks done, referrals and your x activity. here's what feeds it, going by what the page itself says.

x activity

  • it counts your public x posts that use #Fomies or mention @FomiesNFT.
  • only your most recent 40 posts are checked. post about other things for a while and older fomies posts fall outside the window.
  • it's re-checked when you open your file, at most once a day. refreshing every five minutes won't move it.
  • the record shows views on recent fomies posts, new views since the last check, and how many recent fomies posts it found. a + means at least that many.
  • protected accounts can't be seen. public posts only.

tasks

they live in the tasks tray in the office. this is what the tray showed on oct 3. tasks get added, paused and expired, so yours may differ.

submit a public complaint
reply to the department's post, and like and repost it. paste your reply's link, then press check.
reinforce the department
boost the telegram channel. optional, telegram premium only, 200 points.
report to telegram
join the telegram channel. you connect telegram once through the department's bot, then check membership.
follow @FomiesNFT
follow, then check.

“the provider's evidence could not be validated.”

the most common error in the tray. it means the service that checks x for the department didn't get back something it could trust. it's usually not about you.

tips, not official

  1. make sure the post is public and from the same x account you signed in with.
  2. paste the post's own link, like x.com/yourname/status/123…. if it keeps failing, try it without the ?s=… bit at the end.
  3. give a brand-new post a few minutes. checkers don't always see it right away.
  4. don't hammer the check button. there's a limit, and the error doesn't leave faster.
  5. try again later. the department's own noticeboard said once that task checks briefly hit their limits, and “if something failed earlier, try it again.”
  6. if the tray says a task changed, refresh it, or close and reopen your file.
other messages the tray can show, and what they mean
“Use a public post from your signed-in X account.”
wrong account, or the post isn't public.
“The post does not directly target the required post.”
reply to the exact post the task names, not to a reply under it.
“Use the required post type. A repost or quote does not count for this task.”
the task wants a reply (or whatever it says), not a quote or repost.
“The post was created outside this task's time window.”
it's older than the task. make a new one.
“The checked relationship does not show you following this account.”
follow first, wait a moment, check again.
“Too many checks. Wait a minute before trying again.”
exactly that.
“The evidence service is busy. Try later.” / “…temporarily unavailable.”
not you. come back later.
“Still checking reposts. Try again shortly.”
it's working on it. give it a minute.
“This task is paused, archived or expired. Refresh the tray.”
the task is gone or on hold.

messages quoted from the site. the explanations are my reading of them, not the department's.

referrals

your folder has a referral link: fomies.family/?ref= plus 24 characters. the count in your record goes up when people sign in through it. what referrals are worth hasn't been said.

pasting your link under every post is how reply farms behave. share it with people who'd actually enjoy the place.

D questions

form fq-1 · questions people keep asking

questions people keep asking

collected from the replies under official posts. answered with what's actually known, which is less than you'd like.

how do i get wl?

you apply on fomies.family and the committee decides. nobody has announced any other route. approvals arrive in batches and show up in your folder. nobody in the replies can get you in, and anyone offering to is selling something.

when is mint? which chain? how many?

not announced. the x bio says “free mint”, and that's the whole official spec so far. the form asks for a solana and an evm address, which tells you they want both, not which chain. dates and supply numbers you see around are guesses unless they come from @FomiesNFT.

my solana address isn't accepted. what do i do?

paste it into the checker. the usual causes are an invisible character from copying, a cut-off copy, or an evm address in the solana box. copy it again with your wallet's own copy button.

where do results show up?

in your folder on fomies.family. the clerk is firm about this: “the folder gets it first. not the bell.”

what's a briefcase? i don't see mine.

briefcases appear in noticeboard memos. “98 briefcases have been packed.” then, later, “a surprising number remain unclaimed.” nothing official has said what's in one or how to claim it yet. watch the official channels.

does ringing the bell help?

no. the bell says “ringing is not a signature.” the noticeboard also once reported that “someone rang the bell 127 times today. we know who you are. this has been added to your file.” take that however you like.

can i change my answers or addresses after filing?

plan as if you can't. once you file, the folder shows a receipt instead of the form. check your addresses before you file. that's what the checker is for.

can i apply twice?

“one active file per account. it keeps the department honest. allegedly.” that's the clerk.

the window shut on me. are applications closed?

they were paused once, from sep 27 to sep 28 (utc), and reopened. if the office is closed, the door says so. reopenings get posted on @FomiesNFT.

does posting more get me in faster?

nobody outside the department knows how files are decided, me included. your record only looks at your last 40 posts. posting the same thing forty times is not a personality.

what was the morse code post?

it decodes to: “IS THIS ON. THE DEPARTMENT HAS OPENED AN OFFICIAL BROADCAST CHANNEL. ANNOUNCEMENTS WILL BE MADE THERE WHEN NECESSARY. UNFORTUNATELY, THEY MAY BE NECESSARY.” the channel is t.me/fomiesofficial.

is this page official?

no. see the stamp at the top.

E glossary

form gl-1 · terms used in the building

glossary

case number
your file's number, issued when you file. it's on your id card and at the top of your folder pages.
access card
the id card in your folder. it shows your photo, alias, classification, status and number. it says visitor before you file and applicant after. the department's posts talk about access cards being handed out a few at a time, so people often use the word to mean getting approved. nothing official spells out the difference.
pass
two meanings. before you file, your card shows a pass number and the note “must be escorted at all times.” separately, an access pass was the prize in the oct 2 telegram raid.
briefcase
something the department packs for decided cases, according to the noticeboard. contents not public.
the clerk
the intake officer, window three. suit, big eyes, desk, bell. he runs your interview and would like you to stop ringing.
the committee
whoever actually decides files. you never meet them. the clerk doesn't either.
folder
your case file. it holds your activity record, tasks, card and, eventually, a decision.
the vent
the grate in the hallway. something with eyes lives behind it and passes along building gossip. see things to try.
the bell
on the clerk's desk. ringing it gets you a remark, not a result.
noticeboard
the board of memos in the office. new notes replace old ones, so the timeline keeps a few.
end of thread card
a pinned card the official account posts under its own posts: “the department has nothing further to add. any reply below this claiming otherwise is a scam.” the team doesn't reply to people.
alias
the name the clerk calls you. up to 20 characters.
pickles
HR recommends not asking about the pickles.
classification
the personality label your interview gives you. there are six:
  • the professional lurker reads everything. posts when absolutely necessary.
  • the meme department communicates primarily through images.
  • the vibe inspector notices immediately when something feels off.
  • the lorekeeper remembers announcements the team has forgotten.
  • the reply goblin somehow already in the replies.
  • the question mark difficult to classify. apparently staying anyway.
descriptions as written on the site.
F things to try

form sx-1 · things to try in the office

things to try in the office

small things hidden around the building. spoilers, so they're folded. a few need a mouse, because you can't hover on a phone. a few need sound.

01 the vent peeks

in the hallway, hover over the vent and hold still for about a second. it peeks out at you.

02 the vent has news

click the vent. it shares its news one item at a time, in order: the elevator, the building work, a new voice that “arrived with a chair”, the desk, and a stamp heard from the other room. your browser remembers which ones you've heard.

after that, the conversations depend on where your application is: signed out, filed, decided and so on. ask it where the elevator goes. “up, probably. nobody's let me check.”

03 the bell knows your status

ring it a few times in a row. the clerk works through a set of lines, then starts improvising. the lines change with your status. signed out, you get “the bell is not an application.” once filed, “you've done your part. this is the part where nothing happens.” pause for a few seconds and he resets.

04 the locked door has feelings

when the office is closed, click the door three times within two seconds. sound on. it gets angry.

05 music through the bathroom wall

hover over the bathroom door with sound on. something is playing in there. the maintenance memo insists “there is nothing unusual about the room.”

06 the barcode is real

the barcode on your id card isn't decoration. it encodes your number: your case number once you've filed, your pass number before.

07 the staff wall skips a number

the staff photos go fomie 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, then 7. nobody has said where 6 went.

H scams

form sc-1 · scam alerts

scam alerts

the department is slow. scammers are not. these patterns keep turning up under fomies posts.

END OF THREAD

THE DEPARTMENT HAS NOTHING FURTHER TO ADD.
ANY REPLY BELOW THIS CLAIMING OTHERWISE IS A SCAM.

what the official account pins under its posts. my recreation, their words.

  1. there is no fomies token.

    @FomiesNFT has never posted a contract address. a “CA” in the replies is not from the department, however official it looks. a typical one asks “is this the right CA?” and then gives an address. it isn't the right ca. there isn't one.

  2. the “DEPARTMENT OF FOMO (DOF)” token on solana is not official.

    someone made a solana token with that name. it was not announced by @FomiesNFT. a matching name is not a connection. its contract, so you can recognize it:

    DpdX1i3NFL5qHJJoRxeiEXm5p2Y1BKS1dwjX8UhdiLuF

  3. “check your dms” replies.

    replies that say “check your DMs. There might be something important for you there.” are not from the team. neither are teasers about a “new version” of fomies. the department posts announcements on its own account and channel, not in replies or dms.

  4. the team never replies.

    every official thread ends with an end of thread card. anyone below it claiming to be the team, a mod or an “official helper” is not.

  5. never connect a wallet. never sign anything.

    the application only asks you to type two addresses. there's no wallet connection, no signature, no fee and no “verification” step. if a site asks you to connect or sign for fomies, close it. nobody legit ever needs your seed phrase or private key.

  6. check the address bar.

    the site is fomies.family. lookalike domains with an extra letter or a different ending are an old trick. the x sign-in should happen on x.com.

  7. don't trust addresses other people send you.

    lookalike characters from other alphabets can make a fake address look right. the checker flags them.

this section describes patterns. it doesn't accuse any particular account. when in doubt, use the official links at the top.
